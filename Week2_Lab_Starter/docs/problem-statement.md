# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Patients, doctors, healthcare professionals, clinic administrators, and healthcare facilities are affected by difficulties in managing healthcare appointments. Patients may struggle to find available appointment slots, while clinic staff must handle appointment bookings, cancellations, and patient enquiries manually.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Traditional appointment booking methods often require patients to make phone calls or visit clinics to schedule appointments. This process can be time-consuming and may lead to double bookings, missed appointments, scheduling conflicts, and delays in receiving appointment confirmations. Patients may also have difficulty finding suitable appointment times and accessing appointment information.

## Why does it matter?
These problems reduce the efficiency of healthcare services and increase the administrative workload of clinic staff. Patients may experience inconvenience, longer waiting times, and difficulty accessing medical consultations. An inefficient appointment management process can also affect patient satisfaction and the overall quality of healthcare services.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Many healthcare facilities still rely on traditional appointment booking methods, making it difficult for patients to schedule and manage their medical appointments efficiently. Patients often experience difficulties finding available appointment slots, while clinic staff face administrative challenges such as scheduling conflicts, double bookings, and appointment cancellations. These issues increase waiting times, create additional workloads, and reduce patient satisfaction. Therefore, an AI Healthcare Appointment Assistant is proposed to simplify appointment scheduling, provide intelligent appointment assistance, and improve the overall efficiency of healthcare appointment management.
